# Surface brief: Home (/) and /services/reports-and-presentations — Direction A

Mode: Experience on the work regions, Persuade on hero and close. Audience: agency CDs and in-house B2B marketing leads; judge quality first. Action: tell Nathan what's coming up (form in the close, button in the hero).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Duet's page, finished like a printed monograph: the work sits on plates of its own colour that run to the page edge, words sit on paper. Refuses the centred hero, the card grid and the repeated label-left split.

OWN-WORLD: Warm grey paper #f4f2ee, ink #1c1c1a, one muted ink #615e59, hairline rules #d1ccc4. No accent colour: the client work is the only colour. Zilla Slab 600 for large headlines only; Schibsted Grotesk for all else. Square corners, run-in captions (bold name, muted text), underlined text links, solid ink buttons.

STORY: Visitor sees a senior headline and a real report on its own purple plate in the first screen, scrolls a staggered set of five projects, picks one of four kinds of project, and writes a few lines in the dark close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Text nav. Headline at 104px over two lines, cols 1–11. Below it, standfirst cols 1–6 and, cols 8–12, the primary button "Tell me what's coming up", "See the work" link and client credit. From ~600px down, the Pendulum report on a full-bleed #2c233e plate.

FORM: Duet's layout pinned and pushed; seed: none (pinned) "Start from it and push it further. Keep Duet's identity recognisable".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
