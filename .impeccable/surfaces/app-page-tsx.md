## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a small gallery hang of the studio's work, not a portfolio grid. Each piece is hung the way its medium lives: a report as an open spread, a campaign as a run of ads on one centre line, a book as an object, a site as a working screen. It refuses the uniform thumbnail grid and the centred template hero.

OWN-WORLD: Painted gallery rooms. Wall colours: Urbane Bronze (#54504A) for the main rooms, a cool pale stone (#DDE2E1) for reading rooms, a deep slate (#2F3A40) for the close. Sea-foam aqua (#9FE5D2) only on the primary action and focus. Museum wall labels (name bold, medium line, one sentence) are the caption system. Works hang on a shared centre line, never top-aligned. No frames, no spotlights, no texture.

STORY: The visitor walks in, reads the exhibition title, sees the Pendulum spread at full scale, walks through rooms of work, reaches the information desk (four services), then the contact form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Bronze wall. Zilla Slab title top-left across about two thirds of the width. Pendulum spread hung right, about 55% wide, top just under the title's first line, bottom near the fold. Intro text, aqua button and See the work link low-left at the spread's centre line. Client credit line under the intro.

FORM: Gallery rooms with wall labels, candidate 6 of 7 on my resonance-ordered list, fused with the native-format presentation idea (candidate 1). seed:4aa4865c9d4a91e0 (roll ran degraded: no challengers).
